Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Glow Cart

At Glow Cart, customer satisfaction is our priority. We offer a 7-day return window on all products, including discounted and promotional purchases, subject to the conditions outlined below.

Exchange Policy

  • All products are eligible for return within 7 days of delivery.
  • For change-of-mind returns, products must be unused, unwashed, undamaged and in their original condition.
  • Original packaging, accessories and tags should be included wherever applicable.
  • Sale items and products purchased using promotional coupons are also eligible for returns.
  • Customers must provide their order ID when submitting a return request.
  • Products that are damaged, defective, incorrect or not as described can be returned in accordance with applicable consumer protection requirements.

Exchange Policy

  • Exchange requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Exchanges may be requested for a different size, colour or variant of the same product, wherever available.
  • The product must be in its original condition for change-of-mind exchanges.
  • Exchange requests are subject to product availability and verification.
  • If the requested replacement variant is unavailable, customers may choose an eligible return and refund instead.

Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

  • If you receive a damaged, defective, incorrect or significantly misrepresented product, please raise a request as soon as possible.
  • Customers are encouraged to upload clear photographs or videos showing the issue.
  • Our team will review the complaint and arrange an appropriate resolution, which may include replacement, exchange or refund.
  • Return shipping charges will not be deducted when the issue is attributable to Glow Cart or the seller.
  • We may arrange product pickup or request the product to be returned for inspection, where necessary.

Refund Policy

  • Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received and successfully verified.
  • For eligible change-of-mind returns, the actual reverse courier charges incurred will be deducted from the refundable amount.
  • No return-shipping deduction will apply to eligible returns involving damaged, defective, incorrect or misrepresented products attributable to the seller.
  • Refunds for prepaid orders will be initiated to the original payment method wherever supported.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds will be processed through UPI or bank transfer using the customer's verified payment details.
  • Approved refunds will be initiated within approximately 5–7 business days after receipt and verification. Bank or payment-provider processing may take additional time.
  • Any applicable refund deductions will be communicated to the customer.

Return Shipping Charges

  • For change-of-mind returns, actual reverse courier charges will be deducted from the refund.
  • Return shipping charges will be based on the actual courier or logistics cost incurred, not a fixed return fee.
  • Applicable deductions will depend on the courier service and shipment details.
  • Customers will not bear return shipping charges for eligible seller-responsible damaged, defective or incorrect products.

How to Raise a Return or Exchange Request

  • Log in to your Glow Cart account.
  • Navigate to My Orders in your customer dashboard.
  • Locate the relevant order and select the available Return / Exchange option.
  • Select the request type and provide the reason for your request.
  • Upload photographs or other supporting information wherever required.
  • Submit your request. Our team will review it and communicate the next steps.

Important Conditions

  • Products must not be damaged due to misuse, mishandling or negligence by the customer.
  • All returned products are subject to verification before refund or exchange approval.
  • Customers should retain the order confirmation and return request details until the process is completed.
  • Glow Cart reserves the right to review requests where the returned product does not match the original order or the stated return reason.
  • Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights or remedies available under applicable Indian law.
